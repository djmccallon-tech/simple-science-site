About Simple Science

Simple Science takes new, significant scientific papers and turns them into short, accurate explainers for people with no science background. We write for a curious adult, not a researcher — someone who wants to know what was found and why it matters, without wading through a paper or a press release.

Our promises

  • Every article is written from the primary paper — never a lightly reworded press release.
  • Every article gets a confidence badge (early evidence, well-established, or preliminary) so you know how much weight to put on it at a glance.
  • Every article includes "how sure we should be," stated plainly — including study size, method, and limitations.
  • No hype words — not in the body, and not in the headline either.
  • 400–600 words, every time — a real read time promise, not "however long it takes."
  • If we get something wrong — a study is retracted, fails to replicate, or is contradicted later — we'll say so openly.
Simple Science — new, significant research, reworded for everyday readers. No hype, no jargon, no spin.
