About Simple Science

Simple Science takes new, significant scientific publications and turns them into short, accurate explainers for people with no science background. We write for the general audience that is curious — someone who wants to know what was found and why it matters, without the worry of having a lack of context. No overly worded papers or press releases, just important science made simple by those that care.

Our promises

  • Every article is written from the direct source — each article is written from the original publication, never a lightly reworded press release.
  • Every article gets a confidence badge: early evidence, well-established, or preliminary. That way you know how much weight to put on it at a glance.
  • Every article includes "how sure we should be," stated plainly and directly — including the study size, methods, and limitations.
  • No hype words — some sites attempting to simplify research often use exciting words as clickbait. Science is science, we should be excited about all of it!
  • 400–600 words, every time — scientific literature is long and dense; we want our audience to rely on a quick read that can be done in 3–5 minutes.
  • If we get something wrong — a study is retracted, fails to replicate, or is contradicted later — we'll say so openly.

Who your writers are

We are a small team of researchers who got tired of not being able to express our love for science in a way that all members of the general public could connect to. Science can be hard to read — our entire goal is to eliminate that issue for you.

Interested in joining the team? Simple Science is strictly volunteer-based. If you have a background in scientific research or studies, and have been searching for an opportunity to spread important information to more people, then you belong here. Reach out to hello@thesimplescience.com and a member of our team will respond in 1–2 business days.
